What the free download is

Vayora Tickets Free is a single file that runs in your web browser on one computer. You don't need an account, and you don't need the internet to use it. It prints the same shelf tags and promo tickets as the online version.

  • Your products, print queue and batches are saved on that computer only, inside the browser. Back them up regularly (see Backup and moving computers).
  • It's free to use for as long as you like. Tickets are clean for the first 60 days. After that, each ticket carries a small "Printed free with Vayora Tickets · vayora.au" line.
  • Plus (A$49/year) or Lifetime (A$99 once) removes that line and adds extra features.
Need several devices or staff logins? The online version syncs between computers and phones, with a 30-day free trial.

Download and open it

  1. On vayora.au, click Download free. You get one file: Vayora Tickets Free.html.
  2. Move it somewhere permanent, e.g. your Documents folder or Desktop.
  3. Double-click it. It opens in your browser. Google Chrome or Microsoft Edge works best.
  4. Type your store's name into Business name.
Always open the same file in the same browser. Your data is kept in that browser, so opening the file in a different browser shows an empty copy. Don't clear the browser's "site data" without exporting a backup first.

The 60 clean days, and after

For your first 60 days, tickets print clean. The line under the app name counts down the days. When they're up:

  • Every ticket gets a small line at the bottom: "Printed free with Vayora Tickets · vayora.au".
  • Promo tickets (A7, A5, A4) also get a light diagonal "Vayora Tickets Free" mark.
  • Everything else keeps working. Nothing is locked or deleted.

Buying and activating a licence

  1. Click Get Plus or Get Lifetime. The Vayora licence page opens in your browser (this step needs the internet).
  2. Check your store name (it goes on your licence), add your email for the receipt, and click Continue to secure payment. Pay on Stripe's secure page.
  3. Your licence key is shown straight after payment. Click Copy key, and keep a copy, e.g. with Save as a file or in your email.
  4. Back in the app, click ⭐ Upgrade, paste the key under Your licence and click Activate key.
Plus renews yearly. Stripe charges your card each year, and you can cancel any time. When your key is close to its date, the app shows Renew key. One click gets your updated key. To see invoices or cancel, click the plan badge, then Invoices & billing.

To manage your licence later, click the Plus or Lifetime badge next to the app name. You can refresh the key, open invoices and billing, or remove it from this computer.

Plus features

These are in Fields, under Plus features.

  • Store logo: click Choose logo… and pick a PNG or JPG. It prints in the corner of promo tickets, and on shelf tags too if you switch that on.
  • Promo ticket colours: Standard, Sale red, Clearance yellow, Special green, Blue, or My colours. Set a default here, or choose per ticket in the Print Queue (Colour under the promo details).
  • Shelf tag text sizes: make product names or prices bigger or smaller.
  • Saved layouts: save your fields, paper and sizes under a name (e.g. "Vision 30UP shelf tags") and switch with Use.

Auto-backup to a folder

In 💾 Backup / Restore, click Choose backup folder… and pick a folder, ideally a USB stick or a OneDrive / Google Drive folder. A backup file is then saved there automatically after every change, one per day, kept for 30 days. Your browser asks for permission again after a restart: click Resume auto-backup on the banner. This needs Chrome or Edge.

Lifetime features

Price-change import

When you import a new price file, the app lists every product whose price changed. Click Add these to the print queue to print only those tickets. You don't have to hunt through the catalog.

Sales calendar

Click 📅 Sales to see promo tickets (from your queue and saved batches) that start or end in the next 7 days, and sales that have ended. One click queues normal-price shelf tags for ended sales, so the old specials come down on time. A banner reminds you when sales are starting or ending.

Up to 3 store profiles

Run more than one shop? Use the store menu in the green bar and choose ＋ Add a store…. Each store has its own catalog, queue, batches, settings and logo. Switch between them from the same menu.

Backup and moving computers

Because everything is saved on this computer only, a backup file is your safety net.

  • Export backup (.json) saves everything (products, queue, batches, settings) and your licence into one file. Do this weekly, or turn on auto-backup with Plus.
  • New computer: copy the app file and your latest backup across. Open the app, go to Backup / Restore and choose Choose backup file to restore. Your data and licence come with it.
  • Backups contain your licence key, so keep them private.
  • Export catalog (Excel) exports just your products, ready to import into the online version.

Refer a store

Click 🎁 Refer a store and send your link to another shop. They get a 60-day free trial of the online version. When they subscribe, you get 3 months of Plus free, or 3 more months if you already have Plus. Click Check my rewards to collect them; the reward comes as a licence key to paste in.

Moving to the online version

  1. In 💾 Backup / Restore, click Export catalog (Excel).
  2. Start a free trial at tickets.vayora.au (see the online guide).
  3. In the online version, use Import Excel / CSV with that file. The columns match automatically.

Troubleshooting

My products have disappeared

Make sure you've opened the same file in the same browser as before. If the browser's data was cleared, restore your latest backup from Backup / Restore.

"That key isn't valid"

Copy the whole key. It starts with VT1. and is quite long. Paste it without adding anything. If you saved it as a file, open the file and copy the key line.

My Plus licence says it ran out

Once your yearly payment has gone through, click Renew key on the banner (or click the badge → Renew / refresh key) to get your updated key, then paste it in.

The buttons for Plus or Lifetime don't open anything

Buying, renewing and collecting rewards open the Vayora website, so you need an internet connection for those steps. Everything else works offline.

Barcodes won't scan at the register

Print at 100% / Default scale, not "Fit to page".
