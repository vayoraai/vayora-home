Built for a busy retail floor

Small tickets for small products

Print on small label sheets like Avery L7157 (33 per sheet) or L7651 (65), small pre-cut ticket sheets, or a dense 4×10 grid on plain A4. Long names shrink to fit.

RRP struck through

Switch on the MRP field and the recommended price prints struck through beside your price, so the saving is obvious on every shelf.

Catalogue sales

Scan each catalogue line and set the offer as you go: % off with was/now prices, NOW $X or 2 FOR $X, with the sale dates printed on the ticket.

Consistent across the team

Everyone prints from the same catalog and the same layout, so tickets look the same whoever made them.

Assistants scan, you print

Staff open the app on their phone, tap "I'm on staff" and enter your store code and name. No accounts to set up. As they scan bays, tickets appear in the print queue on the dispensary or office computer, tagged with who scanned them.

Before the catalogue starts, save the queue as a batch (e.g. "November catalogue"). Reprint it any time a ticket goes missing.

Questions

Can I print small shelf tickets for small products?

Yes. Use small label sheets, small pre-cut sheets or a dense A4 grid. Very narrow tickets are marked "⚠ narrow" so you can test-scan one sheet first.

Can tickets show the RRP and our price?

Yes. Switch on the MRP field in Fields.

Can I bring in products from our dispensary or POS system?

If it can export an Excel or CSV file with a name, barcode and price column, you can import it. Columns are matched for you.

How much does it cost?
