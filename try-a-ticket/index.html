Tickets you can make here

  • Shelf tag: product name, size, price, optional was price and aisle, and a scannable barcode. 24 fit on an A4 sheet (3 across, 8 down).
  • A7 promo ticket (74 × 105 mm): an offer banner such as "20% OFF" or "2 FOR $5.00", the sale price with the old price struck through, and sale dates. 8 per A4 sheet.
  • A5 promo ticket (148 × 210 mm): the same layout, larger, 2 per sheet. Good for shelf-edge specials and end caps.
  • A4 promo ticket: a full-page specials sign for windows, counters and pallets.

These are the same ticket layouts the Vayora Tickets app prints. See more about promo ticket sizes and shelf ticket paper.

Printing tips

  • Use any A4 laser or inkjet printer and plain A4 paper.
  • In the print window, choose 100% or Actual size, not "Fit to page", so tickets come out the right size.
  • Cut on the dashed lines. A guillotine or paper trimmer gives the neatest edges.
  • A7 promo tickets print on a landscape sheet. Your browser should pick landscape for you.

What printer and paper do I need?

Any ordinary A4 laser or inkjet printer and plain A4 paper. Print at 100% (actual size) and cut on the dashed lines.
