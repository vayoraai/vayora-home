Print on pre-cut sheets in four steps

  1. Pick your paper. Click the 🧾 paper button and choose your sheet, for example Vision Supply 30UP.
  2. Print the alignment test page once. Hold it against a real sheet up to the light to check every ticket sits inside its box.
  3. Fine-tune if needed. If the sizes are right but the whole sheet sits slightly off, the Fine-tune arrows shift it in 0.5mm steps. Printers differ by a millimetre or two, which is normal.
  4. Print at 100%. In the print window choose A4 and scale Default or 100%. Never "Fit to page".
Barcodes not scanning? They're printed at true size so POS scanners can read them. "Fit to page" or a shrunk scale makes them too small.

Tickets that run to the edge of the sheet keep their text away from the edge automatically, because most printers can't print the outer few millimetres. Long product names shrink to fit.

Paper not on the list?

Choose My paper isn't listed and grab a ruler:

  • the width and height of one ticket,
  • how many tickets across and down,
  • the distance from the top and left edge of the sheet to the first ticket.

The app works out the gaps and shows a live drawing. Save it to My papers and it's there next time.

Questions

Can I print shelf tickets on Vision Supply sheets?

Yes. Vision Supply 24UP, 30UP and 33UP are built in. Pick the sheet, print the alignment test page once, then print your tickets.

Does it work with POS'99 shelf ticket paper?

Yes. POS'99 33UP sheets are built in as a template.

Which Avery or Officeworks label sheets are supported?

L7173, L7165, L7163, L7162, L7161, L7160, L7159, L7157 and L7651, and compatible labels with the same layout.

Do I need a special printer?

No. Any normal office printer that prints A4 works, inkjet or laser.

Is there a free version?

Yes. Vayora Tickets Free runs on one computer with no account and includes the paper templates. The online version adds live sync across phones and computers for your whole team.
