Re-ticket a bay between customers

  1. On a phone, set the Current bay / aisle, for example "Drinks fridge".
  2. Scan the shelf barcodes with the phone camera. Each one goes into the shared print queue, tagged with the bay and the staff member.
  3. If a product isn't in the catalog yet, the app opens the Add product form with the barcode filled in.
  4. When it's quiet, print from the back office at 100% on A4 and shelve in the order the tickets come out.

Questions

Can staff on different shifts print shelf tickets?

Yes. Staff open the app on their phone, tap "I'm on staff" and enter your store code and their name. Each scan and print is tagged with who did it.

Can I make 2 FOR $X and meal deal tickets?

Yes. Promo tickets support 2 FOR $X, % off, NOW $X with the old price struck through, or your own text, in A7, A5 or A4.

Do I need a special ticket printer?

No. Any office printer that prints A4, inkjet or laser.
