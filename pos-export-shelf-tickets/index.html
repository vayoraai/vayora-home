From POS file to printed tickets

  1. In your POS, export your products or price list as Excel (.xlsx) or CSV. Most systems, including Square, Lightspeed and Shopify POS, have an export option for the product or item list.
  2. In Vayora Tickets, click Import Excel / CSV and choose the file.
  3. Check the columns. Barcode, name and price are usually matched automatically, and you can point any column at the right field.
  4. Click Import products. Then scan or tick what needs a ticket and click Preview & Print.

Why import instead of typing

No typos on the shelf

The price on the ticket is the price in your POS, so the shelf and the register agree.

Barcodes that scan

EAN-13, UPC and EAN-8 barcodes print as proper retail barcodes, so staff can scan the shelf edge to re-ticket.

Price changes in one step

Import the new file and products are matched by barcode and updated, not duplicated, so your catalog stays current.

Works with any POS

There's no connection to your POS to set up or break. If it exports Excel or CSV, it works.

What to include in the export

Only three columns are needed. The rest are optional extras for the ticket.

  • Needed: barcode (or SKU), product name, price.
  • Optional: size or pack (e.g. 2L, 6-pack), unit price (per kg / per 100g), category.
Tip: if your POS exports thousands of lines, import the whole file once. After that, scan the shelf or search the catalog to queue just the tickets you need.

Print on the paper you already use

Plain A4 to cut, pre-cut shelf ticket sheets (Vision Supply, POS'99 and others) or Avery / Officeworks labels. See all supported paper, and make specials tickets from the same products.

Questions

Which POS systems work with Vayora Tickets?

Any POS that can export its product list as Excel (.xlsx) or CSV. You match the columns once when you import, so the column names don't need to be exact.

How do I update prices after a price change?

Export a fresh file from your POS and import it again. Products are matched by barcode and updated, not duplicated.

Do I need to connect Vayora Tickets to my POS?

No. There is no integration to set up: you export a file and import it.
