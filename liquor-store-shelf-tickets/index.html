Was / now and % off

Sale dates on every ticket

Reprint next month

Save each promotion as a batch and reprint it in one click when it comes around again.

Ticket a promotion in one walk

  1. Choose Promo A7 for the shelf edge, A5 for stacks and end displays, or A4 for the window.
  2. Tick Ask for offer on each scan and scan each promoted product. A small box asks for the deal straight after the scan.
  3. Export the offer list to CSV or Excel for your POS or staff, then print at 100% on plain A4.
  4. Save the queue as a batch, for example "November promo", before you clear it.

Everyday shelf tickets too

Regular shelf tags print on plain A4 or pre-cut sheets such as Vision Supply and POS'99, with a scannable barcode on each. See all supported paper and more on promo tickets.

Questions

Can I print multi-buy tickets like 2 for $30?

Yes. Use the Bundle deal offer type, or Custom text for any other wording.

Can tickets show when a promotion ends?

Yes. Sale dates print on the ticket.

Do I need a special printer?

No. Any normal office printer that prints A4, inkjet or laser.
