From product list to shelf in five steps

Here's the whole workflow, using screenshots from the real app with a demo store.

  1. 1 Import

    Import your products from Excel or CSV

    Upload the product export from your POS: Square, Lightspeed, Shopify POS or any system that saves a spreadsheet. Common column names like Description, EAN and Sell Price are matched for you. Check them and import.

    Matching barcodes update the existing product, so a new price file never creates duplicates. How POS import works

  2. 2 Scan

    Scan barcodes on the shop floor

    Walk the aisle with a phone or a USB / Bluetooth scanner. Each scan finds the product instantly and adds it to the print queue, tagged with the aisle or bay you're working in.

    Staff join with your store code and their name. They don't need their own account.

  3. 3 Queue

    Build one shared print queue

    Everyone's scans land in the same queue, live on every phone, tablet and laptop. Set copies, the ticket type and specials like % off, 2 for $X, NOW $X or was/now pricing as you go.

    Save a print run as a batch so you can re-print it next month.

  4. 4 Preview

    Preview every ticket before you print

    See the exact sheet that will come out of the printer: product name, size, aisle, price and a scannable EAN barcode on every ticket.

    Choose which fields appear and pick your paper once. The layout fits the sheet automatically.

  5. 5 Print

    Print shelf tickets and promo tickets on A4

    Print shelf tags on plain A4 and cut, or on pre-cut ticket sheets. Specials print true to size as A7, A5 or A4 promo tickets, with the offer banner, sale dates and was/now price.

    These are real sheets from the app: eight A7 promo tickets on one A4 page, and a sheet of shelf tags.

Questions

Step-by-step help with pictures: read the store guide.

What do I need to start the free trial?

An email address and your business's ABN. No card. You'll get an email to confirm your address, then your store is ready.

Can I see it before I sign up?

Yes. Book a free 15-minute demo and we'll show you the import, scanning and printing steps with your kind of products. Or download the free version and try it on one computer with no account.

What happens when the trial ends?

The app asks you to subscribe before you can keep scanning, importing or printing. Nothing is deleted: your products, print queue and saved batches are all there when you subscribe.

Can I start another free trial?

Free trials are one per business. If an ABN, account, device or internet connection has already had a trial, a new store goes straight to the subscription page. Everything else works the same. If that's happened by mistake (for example a shared office connection), just contact us and we'll sort it out.

Do my staff need their own accounts?

No. Staff open the app on their phone, tap "I'm on staff", and enter your store code and their name. Scans and prints are tagged with who did them. Managers who need full access can be invited by email.

What printer and paper do I need?

Any normal A4 office printer, laser or inkjet. Print on plain A4 and cut, or use pre-cut / perforated shelf ticket sheets. Presets are built in for common Australian stock, plus an alignment test page. See every supported paper (Vision Supply, POS'99, Avery labels).

Is there a free version?

Yes. Download Vayora Tickets Free. It runs on one computer with no account. Tickets are clean for the first 60 days; after that each ticket carries a small "Printed free with Vayora Tickets" line. Plus (A$49/year) or Lifetime (A$99 once) removes it and adds extras like your logo on tickets.

Is Vayora Tickets Australian?

Yes. Vayora Tickets is developed in Sydney for Australian independent retailers. Prices are in Australian dollars and the paper presets cover ticket stock sold in Australia.

How does "Refer a store" work?

Share your link from "🎁 Refer a store" in the app. The store you refer gets a 60-day free trial instead of 30. When they pay for their subscription, you get a free month, taken off your next bill or added to your trial. Free-download users get 3 months of Plus instead.

Can I cancel?

Yes, any time. You keep access until the end of the period you've paid for.
