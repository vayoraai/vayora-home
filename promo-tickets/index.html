Questions

What size are A7, A5 and A4 promo tickets?

A7 is 74×105mm (8 per A4), A5 is 148×210mm (2 per A4) and A4 is a full page. All print true-size on plain A4.

Do I need special paper or a special printer?

No. Any normal office printer that prints A4 works, inkjet or laser. For shelf tags on pre-cut sheets, see supported shelf ticket paper.

Do promo tickets have barcodes?

Yes, a scannable EAN barcode at true size on every ticket.

Can I reprint last month's specials?

Yes. Save the queue as a batch (e.g. "Weekly specials — 21 Oct") and reprint it any time.

Is there a free version?

Yes. Vayora Tickets Free prints the same promo tickets on one computer. See above for what happens after 60 days.
