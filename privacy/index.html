Privacy

Privacy and cookies

Vayora Tickets is developed in Sydney, NSW. This page explains, in plain English, what information we keep, why, and the choices you have. Last updated 25 November 2026.

What the online app stores

When you start a free trial at tickets.vayora.au we store:

  • Your sign-in email address, so you can sign in and so we can contact you about your account.
  • Your store name and ABN, to set up your store and check that each business gets one free trial.
  • Your products, print queue and saved batches, so your team sees the same data on every device.
  • Staff names that your staff enter when they join with your store code, so scans and prints show who did them.
  • How you found us: the campaign tags (for example utm_source=facebook) or the name of the website that linked to us, if any. This contains no personal details.
  • Setup milestones: the dates of your store's first import, first scan and first print, so we can see where new stores get stuck and improve the app.

To make sure each business gets only one free trial, the app also checks a scrambled (hashed) copy of the ABN, the account, the device and the internet connection used to start a trial.

Your data is stored with Google Firebase. Payments are handled by Stripe. We never see or store your card number.

Google Analytics (optional)

With your permission, we use Google Analytics 4 to count visits, see which websites and campaigns bring people to us, and see which buttons get used, such as "Start free trial", "Download free" and "Book a demo". This helps us decide what to improve.

  • Google Analytics only loads after you click Accept. If you click No thanks, nothing is sent to Google.
  • We never send your name, email address, ABN, phone number, store name or store code to Google.
  • Google signals and advertising features are switched off.
  • Web addresses are sent without their search parameters, apart from campaign tags.

You can change your mind at any time: Cookie settings.
