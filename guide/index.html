What Vayora Tickets does

Vayora Tickets prints shelf price tags and promotional tickets with scannable barcodes, from your product list, on any normal office printer. It runs in your web browser at tickets.vayora.au, so there's nothing to install.

Your catalog, print queue and saved batches are stored online and sync live. Staff can scan products on their phones while you print from the computer in the back office.

What you need

  • A computer with Chrome, Edge or Safari, and a printer that prints A4.
  • Your product list as an Excel or CSV file. Most POS systems can export one.
  • Optional: a USB or Bluetooth barcode scanner, or just your phone's camera.
  • Optional: pre-cut shelf ticket sheets such as Vision Supply or POS'99. Plain A4 works too.

Start your free trial

  1. Go to tickets.vayora.au and choose Start free trial.
  2. Enter your email and a password (at least 6 characters), then click Create account.
  3. Open the email we send you and click the confirmation link. Back in the app, click I've confirmed — continue.
  4. Enter your store name and ABN, then click Start 30-day free trial.
Good to know: the trial is free for 30 days and needs no card. There's one free trial per business. If you were sent a referral link by another store, your trial is 60 days.

Next time, just open tickets.vayora.au and sign in. Forgot your password? Use Forgot your password? on the sign-in screen.

Add your products

Import an Excel or CSV file

  1. Export your product list from your POS or supplier as Excel (.xlsx) or CSV. It needs at least a name, barcode and price column.
  2. Click Import Excel / CSV and choose the file.
  3. Check the columns are matched correctly (most are matched automatically), then click Import products.
  • Products whose barcode is already in your catalog are updated, not duplicated. Re-importing your price file each week keeps prices current.
  • Rows without a name, barcode or price are skipped.
  • Remove products not in this file is for a full catalog replacement. Leave it off for partial updates.

Add a product by hand

Click ＋ Add product, fill in the name, barcode and price (plus anything else you want on the ticket), and save.

Build your print queue

Everything you want to print goes into the Print Queue first. There are three ways to add to it:

  • From the catalog: set the number of copies and click ＋ next to the product.
  • USB or Bluetooth scanner: click USB / BT scanner and scan. Each scan adds one ticket, and scanning the same item again adds another copy.
  • Phone camera: click Scan with phone, point the barcode inside the box and tap Tap to Scan.
Tip: type a location into Current bay / aisle before you start scanning a section. Every scanned ticket is tagged with it, and the queue is sorted by aisle, so the printed tickets come out in walking order.

Scanned a barcode that isn't in your catalog? The app opens the Add product form with the barcode filled in. These products are also listed under New from scanning, which you can export to add them to your POS.

Choose the type in the toolbar before adding items, or change it for any row in the Print Queue.

Setting an offer

Each promo row in the Print Queue has these promo details:

  • Offer type: % off (e.g. 20% OFF, shows the was/now price), Special price (NOW $4.00), Bundle deal (2 FOR $7.00) or Custom text (e.g. HALF PRICE).
  • Label: a small tag such as "Weekly special".
  • Sale dates: printed as "Valid 28 Oct – 3 Nov".

Scanning a whole promotion? Choose the promo type in the toolbar and tick Ask for offer on each scan. After every scan, a small box asks for the offer.

Need a list of this week's offers for your POS or staff? With promo tickets in the queue, use Export offers CSV or Export offers Excel under the Print Queue.

Preview and print

  1. Click Preview & Print. You'll see exactly how the sheets will print, with the number of tickets and pages.
  2. Click Print now.
  3. In the print window, choose your printer, A4 paper, and scale Default or 100%. Don't use "Fit to page". Then print.
Barcodes not scanning? They're printed at true size so POS scanners can read them. Printing at "Fit to page" or a shrunk scale makes them too small, so always print at 100%.

Choose your paper and fields

Click Fields to choose what appears on shelf tags (MRP struck through, unit, category, bay/aisle, best-before) and which paper you print on.

  • Plain A4: set columns × rows (e.g. 3 × 8) and cut the tags yourself.
  • Pre-cut sheets: choose your stock (Vision Supply 24UP/30UP/33UP, POS'99 33UP) or Custom to enter your own measurements.
  • With pre-cut stock, print 🖨️ Print alignment test page first and hold it against a real sheet up to the light. If everything is the right size but slightly off, adjust nudge right / nudge down by a millimetre or two.

Save and reprint batches

Once your queue is ready, click Save as batch under the Print Queue. Give it a name (e.g. "Weekly specials — 21 Oct") and, if you like, an aisle, bay or zone. Usually the queue is cleared afterwards so you can start the next run.

  • Preview & Print on a batch reprints it any time, and marks it as printed.
  • Change format… switches every ticket in a batch to another type, e.g. reprint your A7 specials as A4 signs.

Staff and managers

Click Staff / members to see who has access to your store.

Staff (no account needed)

  1. Give staff your store code, shown in the store bar (e.g. GVG001).
  2. On their phone or computer, they open tickets.vayora.au, tap I'm on staff — join with a store code, and enter the code and their name.
  3. Their scans and prints are tagged with their name. To remove someone, click Remove next to their name.

Managers and co-owners

For someone who needs full access (settings, staff, subscription), enter their email under Invite another owner / manager. They create an account with that email, confirm it, then choose I was invited and enter your store code.

Troubleshooting

Barcodes won't scan at the register

Print at 100% / Default scale, not "Fit to page". Check the barcode in your product list is the full number on the pack (usually 13 digits). Very worn toner can also cause it.

Tickets don't line up with my pre-cut sheets

In Fields, check the paper preset matches your stock, print the alignment test page and hold it against a sheet. Use the nudge fields for small shifts. Printers differ by a millimetre or two, which is normal.

A long product name is cut short

Text shrinks automatically to fit. On very small tags, the name is limited to two lines. Shorten the name in the catalog if you need all of it, or use a bigger ticket type.

Staff can't join

Check they're entering the store code exactly as shown in your store bar, and choosing "I'm on staff" (not signing in with an email).

I didn't get the confirmation email

Check your spam or junk folder, then use Resend email. Make sure you signed up with the right address.

My store opened on the payment page straight away

Free trials are one per business. If your ABN, account, device or internet connection has already had a trial, a new store starts on the subscription. If that's a mistake (e.g. a shared office connection), email us and we'll sort it out.

Can I use it on more than one computer or phone?

Yes. Sign in (or join as staff) on any device. Everything syncs live.
