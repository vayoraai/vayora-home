Made for how a grocery store runs

Weekly price changes

Export your price file from the POS and import it. Existing products are updated, not duplicated, so one import a week keeps every ticket current.

Per-kg and unit prices

Bring in a unit column with your products and switch it on, so loose fruit, deli and bulk lines show the price per kg.

Printed in aisle order

Type the bay or aisle before scanning a section. Every ticket is tagged with it and the queue sorts by aisle, so tickets come off the printer in walking order.

Weekly specials

A7 tickets for the shelf edge, A5 for end displays and A4 for the window, with % off, NOW $X or 2 FOR $X offers and sale dates.

Re-ticket an aisle with a phone

  1. Set Current bay / aisle, for example "Aisle 3 — Breakfast".
  2. Walk the aisle scanning shelf barcodes with a phone camera or a USB / Bluetooth scanner. Every scan lands in the print queue on every device.
  3. Scanned something that isn't in your catalog? The app opens the Add product form with the barcode filled in, and keeps a list of new items you can export back to your POS.
  4. Print in the back office, at 100% on A4, and shelve in the order they come out.
Staff don't need accounts. They open the app on their phone, tap "I'm on staff" and enter your store code and their name. Scans and prints are tagged with who did them.

Use the paper you already have

Print on plain A4 and cut, or on pre-cut sheets. Vision Supply 24UP / 30UP / 33UP, POS'99 33UP and Avery / Officeworks labels are built in. See all supported paper, or read about promo tickets for specials.

Questions

Can I update shelf ticket prices from my POS each week?

Yes. Import your Excel or CSV price file again. Products are matched by barcode and updated, not duplicated.

Can shelf tickets show a per-kg price?

Yes. Import a unit column with your products, or add it by hand, and switch the unit field on for shelf tags.

What printer do I need?

Any normal office printer that prints A4, inkjet or laser.
