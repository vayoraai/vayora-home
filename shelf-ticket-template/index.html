Template vs Vayora Tickets

No retyping

A template means typing every name and price. Here you import your product list from Excel or CSV once, then scan or search to queue tickets.

Text that fits

Long product names shrink to fit the tag instead of spilling over, and nothing gets cut off at the sheet edge.

Real barcodes

EAN-13, UPC and EAN-8 barcodes print as proper retail barcodes, so staff can scan the shelf edge.

Specials built in

2 FOR $X, % off and NOW $X with the old price struck through, on true-size A7, A5 or A4 tickets.

Every common shelf ticket size

Pick your paper from a picture list instead of setting margins by hand:

  • Plain A4, printed and cut, in grids such as 3×8 or 4×10.

  • Avery / Officeworks label sheets for stick-on tags.
  • Your own sheet: enter the ticket size, rows and columns, and save it.

Questions

Is there a free shelf ticket template?

Yes. Vayora Tickets Free prints shelf tags and specials tickets on one computer with no account. See above for what happens after 60 days.

Can I print shelf tickets with barcodes?

Yes. EAN-13, UPC and EAN-8 barcodes print as proper retail barcodes that POS scanners read.

What sizes can I print?

Shelf tags on plain A4, pre-cut ticket sheets or Avery / Officeworks labels, and specials in true A7, A5 and A4. You can also enter your own measurements.
